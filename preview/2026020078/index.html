Data notes

  • Sources. Game data from the NHL; expected goals from this site’s own model, cross-checked against MoneyPuck.com.
  • Early season. Carolina has played 5 games and Chicago 5, so most numbers blend this season with last. The season switch at the top shows this season alone.
  • Percentiles and ranks compare a team or player with the rest of the league: 90 means better than 90% of the league at that thing.
  • Blind spots. Public game data doesn’t record passes before a shot, where defenders are standing, or rushes up the ice, so the site shows none of those.
  • Hits, giveaways and takeaways depend on each arena’s scorer; anything built on them is arena-adjusted. How it works.
  • Key players will be added once player-level numbers are ready.
