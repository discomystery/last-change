NY Islanders had 30 chances worth 2.3 expected goals. NY Rangers had 38 chances worth 3.6 expected goals.

How it works: every regulation shot that reached the net or missed it is a chance that goes in as often as this site’s expected-goals model says. The game is replayed with those chances, and a replay that ends level after regulation counts as half a win for each team, because overtime and shootouts are close to coin flips. A rebound scramble counts once, since only one shot in it can go in. Empty-net shots are left out.

It knows nothing about goalies or finishing skill, or about passes before a shot. It also leans toward the team that was chasing, because a team with a lead tends to sit back and give up chances. Over the last three seasons, teams it gave 80% or more won about two games in three.
