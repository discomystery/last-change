# The Red Wings erase a two-goal hole and win it in a shootout

Down 3-1 early in the third, the Red Wings came all the way back. Emmitt Finnie cut it to one at 5:00 of the period, and with John Gibson pulled for an extra attacker, Alex DeBrincat tied it with 1:33 left. Lucas Raymond scored the only goal of the shootout, in the third round.

The Sharks built that lead with their power play. Dmitry Orlov answered Justin Faulk's power-play opener late in the first with his first goal of the season, Macklin Celebrini put San Jose ahead early in the second, and Ivar Stenberg made it 3-1 on the power play 2:48 into the third. Celebrini finished with a goal and two assists.

San Jose had the better of the night, 34-27 in shots, and in our replays of the chances they win 57 times in 100. Gibson is why it went the other way: 31 saves, about one and a half goals better than the shots he faced. DeBrincat's tying goal leaves him one short of 300.

- Standings: The Red Wings (3-3-2) climbed off the bottom of the Atlantic. The Sharks (4-3-1) are fourth in the Pacific.
