# Finnie sparks a Red Wings comeback

Justin Faulk got the scoring started for the Red Wings with a power-play goal midway through the first. Late in the period, Keegan Kolesar and Barclay Goodrow fought, and Dmitry Orlov answered on a San Jose power play to tie it. Macklin Celebrini put the Sharks ahead 2-1 early in the second, and Ivar Stenberg made it 3-1 on another power play early in the third. Emmitt Finnie pulled the Wings within one a little over two minutes later, and with the goalie pulled and a minute and a half left, Alex DeBrincat tied it 3-3. Lucas Raymond scored the only goal of the shootout to win it for the Red Wings. Celebrini finished with a goal and two assists in the loss.

The Sharks had a little more of the play and hit a post and the crossbar, but the Red Wings had the finish. John Gibson, who gave up five goals against New Jersey in his last start, bounced back with 31 saves, and Alex Nedeljkovic stopped 24 of 27 at the other end. The Red Wings moved to 3-3-2 and climbed off the bottom of the Atlantic to seventh, and the Sharks fell to 4-3-1 but still climbed to fourth in the Pacific.

- Finally: Dmitry Orlov scored his first goal of the season, his first since Mar 21 (21 games played since).
- Milestone: Alex DeBrincat is now one goal from 300.
- Lineup: Luca Cagnoni was in NHL.com’s projected lineup, but Darnell Nurse played instead.
