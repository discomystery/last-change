# Five points for McDavid as the Oilers pay back the Mammoth

Two days after losing 4-2 to Utah, Edmonton had the rematch put away by the middle of the second. Connor McDavid scored 40 seconds in, and by the time Leon Draisaitl made it 5-0 at 13:48 of the second, McDavid had set up three more. Karel Vejmelka was pulled after that one, with five goals against on 17 shots. McDavid added a second goal in the third for five points, and he has a point in every game this season.

Zach Hyman, back after missing six games, scored on the power play, and Mathieu Joseph scored his first goal as an Oiler.

The score flatters Edmonton. Utah outshot them 28-21, 13-2 in a third period in which John Marino and Mikhail Sergachev scored, and the chances came out dead even in our replays.

- Standings: The Oilers (6-1-1) are second in the Pacific. The Mammoth (6-3-0) are still first in the Central, but their three-game winning streak is over.
- Against them: McDavid and Evan Bouchard both have points in eight straight games against Utah.
