# Five points for McDavid as the Oilers beat the Mammoth

Zach Hyman was back in the lineup after missing six games, and the Oilers wasted no time. Connor McDavid opened the scoring just 40 seconds in, Hyman made it 2-0 on the power play midway through the first, and Mathieu Joseph scored his first goal for Edmonton 32 seconds after that to make it 3-0. Trent Frederic and Jack McBain dropped the gloves late in the first. Kasperi Kapanen and Leon Draisaitl added goals just under three minutes apart midway through the second, and after the fifth, Utah pulled Karel Vejmelka for Sebastian Cossa. John Marino and Mikhail Sergachev scored a minute and a half apart early in the third to cut the lead to 5-2, but McDavid answered a little over four minutes later to make it 6-2 for the Oil. He added three assists for a five-point night.

The chances were closer than the score, and Tristan Jarry made 26 saves for the Oilers. Edmonton moved to 6-1-1, second in the Pacific, and the Mammoth dropped to 6-3-0 but stayed first in the Central.
