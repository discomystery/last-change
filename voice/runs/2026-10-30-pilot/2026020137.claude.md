# Bjorkstrand scores twice as the Rangers stop a two-game slide

The Rangers had this one in hand early. Sean Durzi scored 1:18 in, and J.T. Miller on the power play, Gabe Perreault and Oliver Bjorkstrand made it 4-0 in the second. Bjorkstrand added another early in the third.

Anaheim scored twice in the final 1:09, Nick Jensen in his first game as a Duck and A.J. Greer with 33 seconds left. The shots were even, 31-30 for the Ducks, and Dylan Garand made 29 saves. Alexis Lafrenière had two assists, and Frank Vatrano had one in his 700th NHL game.

- Standings: The Rangers (7-3-1) are second in the Metropolitan.
- Slump: The Ducks (4-4-0) have lost three straight.
