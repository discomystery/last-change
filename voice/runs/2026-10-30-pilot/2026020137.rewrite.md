# Bjorkstrand has two goals as the Rangers take down the Ducks

Nick Jensen made his Ducks debut, after two seasons with the Senators. Sean Durzi struck first for the Rangers a little over a minute in, and J.T. Miller doubled the lead on the power play early in the second. Gabe Perreault and Oliver Bjorkstrand then scored just under three minutes apart midway through the period to make it 4-0, and Bjorkstrand got his second early in the third for a 5-0 lead. By the time Jensen got the Ducks on the board with just over a minute left, the game was long gone, and A.J. Greer’s first goal for Anaheim, with 33 seconds left, only made the final 5-2.

The Rangers had the better of the chances. Dylan Garand made 29 saves, and Ville Husso stopped 25 of 30 at the other end. The Rangers moved to 7-3-1, second in the Metropolitan, while the Ducks fell to 4-4-0, fifth in the Pacific.

- Streak over: Mikael Granlund’s six-game point streak ended.
