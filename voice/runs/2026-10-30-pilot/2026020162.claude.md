# Cooley stops 36, and two early power-play goals hold up for the Flames

Calgary scored twice on the power play in the first, through Aydar Suniev and Joel Farabee, and spent the rest of the night hanging on. Minnesota outshot the Flames 17-3 in the third, Michael McCarron got one back 4:39 into it, and the Wild hit three posts. Devin Cooley stopped 36 of 37, more than two goals better than the shots he faced.

Calgary also killed all four Minnesota power plays. Before the game we gave the Flames a 25% chance; they have now won two straight.

- Standings: The Flames (3-6-0) climbed off the bottom of the Pacific. The Wild (6-3-2) are second in the Central.
- Club history: Jonas Brodin passed Marián Gáborík for eighth on the Wild's all-time assists list, with 219.
- Streak over: Kirill Kaprizov's seven-game point streak ended.
