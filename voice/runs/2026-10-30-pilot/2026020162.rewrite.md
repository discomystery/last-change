# The Flames squeak past the Wild

Aydar Suniev and Joel Farabee scored power-play goals a little over three minutes apart midway through the first to put the Flames up 2-0, and that was nearly enough on its own. Michael McCarron cut the lead to 2-1 early in the third, but Calgary held on.

The Wild had the better of the chances: replay them a hundred times and they win about six in ten. Devin Cooley was the difference, stopping 36 of 37, and the Wild hit three posts on the way to coming up a goal short. Jesper Wallstedt stopped 20 of 22 at the other end.

The Flames also killed all four Wild power plays. They improved to 3-6-0 and climbed off the bottom of the Pacific to seventh, while the Wild dropped to 6-3-2, second in the Central.

- Club history: Jonas Brodin passed Marián Gáborík for eighth on the Wild’s all-time assists list, with 219.
- Old team: Blake Coleman played his first game back in Calgary.
- Streak over: Kirill Kaprizov’s seven-game point streak ended.
- Finally: Michael McCarron scored his first goal of the season, his first since Apr 13 (eleven games played since).
