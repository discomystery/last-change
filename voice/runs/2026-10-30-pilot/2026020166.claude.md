# Evans scores twice, once shorthanded, as the Canadiens beat the Blackhawks

Connor Bedard put Chicago ahead early, from Patrick Kane, and then Jake Evans took over. He tied it with 2:31 left in the first, put Montréal ahead with a shorthanded goal midway through the second and set up Kaiden Guhle's goal with 1:34 left in the period. Mike Matheson's empty-netter, his first goal of the season, finished it.

The Canadiens killed all five Chicago power plays. The chances were closer than the score, and Jacob Fowler made 20 saves. Ian Cole played his 1,000th NHL game for the Blackhawks.

- Standings: The Canadiens (5-3-2) moved up to fifth in the Atlantic. The Blackhawks (3-7-0) are last in the Central.
- Against them: Bedard has a point in all six of his games against Montréal since 2023-24.
