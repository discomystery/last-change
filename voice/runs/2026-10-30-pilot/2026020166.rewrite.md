# Cole plays his 1,000th game, but the Blackhawks lose

Connor Bedard opened the scoring for the Blackhawks early in the first, and it was their only goal of the night. Jake Evans tied it for Montréal late in the period, then struck again shorthanded early in the second to put the Canadiens ahead 2-1. From midway through the second to midway through the third, the Blackhawks went just over 18 minutes without a shot on goal while the Habs put ten on net, and Kaiden Guhle made it 3-1 in that stretch, late in the second. Mike Matheson sealed it with an empty-net goal with less than three minutes left. Evans added an assist for a three-point night.

The chances were closer than the 4-1 score. Jacob Fowler made 20 saves for the Canadiens, and Spencer Knight stopped 24 of 27 for Chicago.

Neither power play scored: the Canadiens killed all five Blackhawks chances, and the Blackhawks killed all three of Montréal’s. The Canadiens moved to 5-3-2 and climbed to fifth in the Atlantic, while the Blackhawks dropped to 3-7-0, still last in the Central.

- Against them: Connor Bedard has a point in all six of his games against Montréal since 2023-24.
- Milestone: The Blackhawks’ Ian Cole played his 1,000th NHL game.
- Finally: Mike Matheson scored his first goal of the season, his first since Mar 31 (twelve games played since).
- The preview: The preview’s call didn’t hold.
