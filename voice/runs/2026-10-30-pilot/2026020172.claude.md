# Rakell ties it late, and Koivunen wins it in overtime

The Penguins spent most of the night in the Predators' end and needed the last minute to show for it. With Artūrs Šilovs pulled, Rickard Rakell tied it 2-2 with 1:06 left, set up by Sidney Crosby and Erik Karlsson, and Ville Koivunen won it 3:44 into overtime.

Nashville led twice. Steven Stamkos opened the scoring midway through the first, his sixth goal in eight games against Pittsburgh since 2023-24, and after Connor Dewar tied it 18 seconds into the second, Jayden Struble scored his first goal as a Predator.

Juuse Saros kept the Predators in it with 34 saves, while the Penguins had 64 shot attempts at 5-on-5 to Nashville's 40. Only two penalties were called all night.

- Standings: The Penguins (5-6-1) have won two straight. The Predators (3-3-4) are winless in two and seventh in the Central.
