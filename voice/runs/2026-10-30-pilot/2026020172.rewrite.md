# Koivunen wins it in overtime

Steven Stamkos got the scoring started for the Predators midway through the first, and Connor Dewar answered for Pittsburgh just 18 seconds into the second to make it 1-1. Jayden Struble scored his first goal for Nashville early in the second to put the Predators back ahead 2-1, and the lead held until Rickard Rakell tied it 2-2 with the goalie pulled and just over a minute left. Ville Koivunen won it in overtime.

The Penguins carried the play, outshooting the Predators 37-26, but needed overtime to win it, because Juuse Saros kept the Preds in it with 34 saves on 37 shots. Artūrs Šilovs made 24 saves at the other end. By our Swing measure, nobody on the ice had a bigger night than Elmer Söderblom, even with Koivunen’s overtime winner in the game.

Only two penalties were called all night, something the league sees about once every 20 games. The Penguins improved to 5-6-1, sixth in the Metropolitan, and the Predators fell to 3-3-4, seventh in the Central.

- Haunts them: Steven Stamkos scored again against Pittsburgh, and has six goals in eight games against them since 2023-24.
- Lineup: Reid Schaefer was in NHL.com’s projected lineup, but Vitali Pinchuk played instead.
- The preview: One of the preview’s two calls held.
