# Tuch and Strome bring the Capitals back, and Tuch ends it in overtime

Buffalo jumped out 2-0 in the first on goals from Jack Quinn and Josh Doan, and then Washington took over. Alex Tuch got one back late in the period on a shot with about a 1% chance of going in, and Dylan Strome scored twice in the second, tying it early and putting the Capitals ahead with 2:36 left in the period. Noah Östlund tied it 2:20 into the third, but Tuch won it 3:42 into overtime, set up by Strome and Jakob Chychrun.

Strome finished with two goals and an assist, and Washington outshot Buffalo 34-21.

- Slump: The Sabres are winless in three (0-1-2) and still last in the Atlantic.
- Standings: The Capitals (6-4-0) moved up to fourth in the Metropolitan.
- Back: Pierre-Luc Dubois returned after missing five games.
