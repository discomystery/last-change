# Tuch sparks a Capitals comeback

Jack Quinn and Josh Doan scored just over four minutes apart early in the first to put the Sabres up 2-0. Alex Tuch, in his first game against Buffalo since leaving, got the Capitals on the board late in the period. Dylan Strome then took over: he tied it 2-2 early in the second and struck again late in the period to put the Caps ahead 3-2. Noah Östlund made it 3-3 for Buffalo early in the third, and Tuch won it in overtime with his second goal of the night. Strome added an assist for a three-point night.

The Capitals owned the chances, outshooting the Sabres 34-21, and still had to sweat it out in overtime, even with the Sabres hitting a post and the crossbar. Charlie Lindgren made 18 saves for the Capitals, and Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen stopped 30 of 34. The Capitals improved to 6-4-0 and climbed to fourth in the Metropolitan, while the Sabres fell to 4-4-3, still last in the Atlantic.

- Slump: The Sabres are winless in three (0-1-2).
- Back: The Capitals’ Pierre-Luc Dubois was back in the lineup after missing five games.
- The preview: The preview’s call didn’t hold.
