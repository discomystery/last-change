# The Stars pull away in the third for their fifth straight win

For two periods, every Dallas lead lasted a few minutes. Matthew Schaefer answered Tyler Seguin's opener, Kyle Palmieri answered Jason Robertson, and Brayden Schenn answered Justin Hryckowian's power-play goal with 2:15 left in the second.

The third was all Dallas. Wyatt Johnston scored 4:10 in, Robertson got his second midway through, and Mikko Rantanen made it 6-3 with 6:58 left. The Stars outshot the Islanders 16-5 in the period and 38-20 overall.

Robertson now has eight goals in seven games against the Islanders since 2023-24, and Rantanen has a point in five straight. Matthew Seminoff and Dylan Hryckowian made their NHL debuts for Dallas.

- Streak: The Stars (7-3-1) have won five straight and are third in the Central.
- Snapped: The Islanders' five-game point streak is over. They are 5-4-2.
