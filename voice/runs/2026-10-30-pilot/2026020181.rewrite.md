# The Stars pour it on late

Tyler Seguin got the scoring started for the Stars midway through the first, and it went back and forth from there. Matthew Schaefer tied it 1-1 for the Islanders a little over two minutes later, and Jason Robertson put the Stars back ahead 2-1 just 49 seconds after that. Kyle Palmieri tied it again early in the second, Justin Hryckowian restored the lead on the power play midway through the period, and Brayden Schenn answered late in the period to make it 3-3. That was the last goal the Islanders would get. Wyatt Johnston gave the Stars the lead early in the third, and Robertson and Mikko Rantanen scored a little over three minutes apart midway through the period to pull away, 6-3.

The Stars carried the play, outshooting the Islanders 38-20, and got the result to match. Jake Oettinger made 17 saves, while Ilya Sorokin allowed six goals on 38 shots. By our Swing measure, Lian Bichsel had the best night of anyone. The Stars improved to 7-3-1, third in the Central, and the Isles fell to 5-4-2, fifth in the Metropolitan.

- Streak: The Stars are rolling: five straight wins.
- Snapped: The Islanders’ five-game point streak is over.
- Haunts them: Jason Robertson scored twice more against the Islanders, and has eight goals in seven games against them since 2023-24.
- Streak: Mikko Rantanen has a point in five straight games.
