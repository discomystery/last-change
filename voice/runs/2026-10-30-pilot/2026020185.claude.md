# Doughty moves up the Kings' list as Los Angeles routs the Rangers

Los Angeles was up 3-1 after one and 5-1 by the middle of the second. Taylor Ward, in his first game of the season after missing nine, scored 1:15 in, and Brandt Clarke, Drew Doughty, Artemi Panarin on the power play and Mikey Anderson followed. James Reimer was pulled after the first period, with three goals against on eight shots.

Doughty finished with a goal and two assists. The goal was his first of the season, and the three points took him past Dustin Brown into seventh on the Kings' all-time scoring list, with 713.

Adam Fox scored on the power play in his 500th NHL game, and the Rangers got within 5-3 late before Joel Edmundson's empty-netter. The chances were closer than the score.

- Head to head: The Kings have beaten the Rangers six straight times, going back to 2023-24.
- Slump: The Rangers (7-6-1) have lost three straight.
