# Doughty has a goal and two assists as the Kings beat the Rangers

The first period was a busy one. Taylor Ward, in his first game of the season, got the scoring started for the Kings a little over a minute in, and Brandt Clarke made it 2-0 less than four minutes later. J.T. Miller got the Rangers on the board midway through the first, but Drew Doughty restored the two-goal lead four and a half minutes after that. The Rangers pulled James Reimer after three goals and sent Dylan Garand out to start the second, and Los Angeles kept coming: Artemi Panarin made it 4-1 on the power play early in the period and Mikey Anderson 5-1 later on. The Kings also had a goal taken off for goalie interference early in the second, though it didn’t end up costing them. Adam Fox, in his 500th NHL game, cut the lead to 5-2 on the power play midway through the period. Tye Kartye made it 5-3 with the goalie pulled and just under four minutes left, but Joel Edmundson put it away with an empty-net goal with less than four minutes left, 6-3. Doughty finished with a goal and two assists.

The chances were closer than the score, and Darcy Kuemper made 23 saves for the Kings. Los Angeles moved to 3-5-2, seventh in the Pacific, and the Rangers fell to 7-6-1, second in the Metropolitan.

- Club history: Drew Doughty passed Dustin Brown for seventh on the Kings’ all-time scoring list, with 713 points.
- Slump: The Rangers are on a three-game losing streak.
- Head to head: The Kings have beaten the Rangers six straight times, going back to the 2023-24 season.
- Finally: Drew Doughty scored his first goal of the season, his first since Mar 16 (24 games played since).
