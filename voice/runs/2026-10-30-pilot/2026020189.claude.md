# Kochetkov ties a Hurricanes record as Carolina takes first from Boston

Pyotr Kochetkov has won all eight of his starts this season, matching Frederik Andersen's 2021-22 run for the best start by a goalie in Hurricanes history. He didn't have to do much in this one: Boston managed 15 shots.

Pavel Zacha scored on the power play 1:31 in, and Jackson Blake tied it with 52 seconds left in the first. From there the Hurricanes outshot the Bruins 30-15, and only Jeremy Swayman kept it level until Sebastian Aho scored midway through the third. Swayman made 28 saves, nearly two goals better than the shots he faced.

The win moved Carolina (10-2-2) past Boston (9-3-1) for the best points percentage in the NHL, and it ended the Bruins' eight-game point streak.

- Last ten: The Hurricanes have won eight of their last ten.
