# Kochetkov wins his first eight, tying a Hurricanes record

Pavel Zacha got the scoring started for the Bruins on the power play a minute and a half in, and that was all they would get. Nicolas Deslauriers and Tanner Jeannot squared off midway through the first, and Jackson Blake tied it for Carolina late in the period. From early in the second to midway through the third, the Bruins went more than 20 minutes without a shot on goal while the Hurricanes put twelve on net, a drought most teams don’t suffer even once a season. Sebastian Aho put the Hurricanes ahead for good midway through the third.

The Hurricanes carried the play, outshooting the Bruins 30-15 (25-9 at 5-on-5), and got the result to match. Pyotr Kochetkov needed only 14 saves for the Canes, while Jeremy Swayman kept the Bruins in it by stopping 28 of 30. Kochetkov has now won all eight of his starts this season, matching Frederik Andersen (2021-22) for the longest winning run to open a season by any goalie in franchise history.

The Bruins did kill off a two-man advantage that lasted less than two minutes late in the second. The Hurricanes improved to 10-2-2 and took the best points percentage in the NHL from the Bruins, who dropped to 9-3-1, so Carolina is now first in the NHL in both points and points percentage.
