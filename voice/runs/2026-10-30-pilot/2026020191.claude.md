# Fowler stops all 46 as the Canadiens blank the Blues

The Blues threw everything at Jacob Fowler, 24 shots in the third period alone, and none of it went in. Fowler finished with 46 saves, about four goals better than the shots he faced, and he has now won six straight starts.

Montréal didn't need much in front of him. Zachary Bolduc scored midway through the second, and Florian Xhekaj made it 2-0 early in the third with his first NHL goal, in his ninth game. Mike Matheson set up both.

St. Louis had the better of the chances: replay them a hundred times and the Blues win about six in ten. Jordan Binnington stopped 18 of 20.

- Streak: The Canadiens (7-3-2) have won three straight and moved up to second in the Atlantic.
- Milestone: Josh Anderson played his 700th NHL game.
- Standings: The Blues (5-6-1) are sixth in the Central.
