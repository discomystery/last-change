# Fowler stops all 46 to blank the Blues

Zachary Bolduc opened the scoring for the Canadiens midway through the second, and early in the third Florian Xhekaj, in his ninth NHL game, scored his first NHL goal to make it 2-0, with Mike Matheson setting him up.

The Blues had the better of the chances, and if you replay them a hundred times, the Blues win about six in ten. Jacob Fowler was the difference, stopping all 46 shots, while Jordan Binnington stopped 18 of 20 at the other end. Fowler has now won six straight starts. The Canadiens moved to 7-3-2 and climbed to second in the Atlantic, and the Blues dropped to 5-6-1, sixth in the Central.

- Streak: Three straight wins for the Canadiens.
- Milestone: The Canadiens’ Josh Anderson played his 700th NHL game.
- Filling in: With Nick Suzuki out, Oliver Kapanen took his spot, though he won only 2 of 7 faceoffs.
- The preview: The preview’s call held.
