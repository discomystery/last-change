# Arvidsson's hat trick sinks the Blackhawks

Connor Bedard gave Chicago the lead in the first, and the Blackhawks were within a goal until late. Viktor Arvidsson tied it midway through the second, Lucas Raymond put Detroit ahead on the power play 1:09 into the third, and Arvidsson finished it himself: a goal with 4:34 left, then an empty-netter with 3:15 left for the hat trick.

John Gibson kept it tied in the second by stopping Frank Nazar on a penalty shot. The Red Wings had the better of the night, 29-19 in shots, and win about seven in ten replays of the chances. Patrick Kane played his first game back in Detroit, now with Chicago.

- Standings: The Red Wings (6-4-3) climbed off the bottom of the Atlantic.
- Road run: The Blackhawks (4-9-0) have lost five straight on the road and are last in the Central.
