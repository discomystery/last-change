# Arvidsson lights the lamp for a hat trick

Connor Bedard opened the scoring for the Blackhawks early in the first, but it was their only goal. Viktor Arvidsson tied it for Detroit early in the second, and midway through the period John Gibson kept it level by stopping Frank Nazar on a penalty shot. Lucas Raymond gave the Red Wings the lead on the power play early in the third, Arvidsson made it 3-1 with four and a half minutes left, and he completed his hat trick into the empty net with a little over three minutes left.

The score was fair: replay this game’s chances a hundred times and the Red Wings win about seven in ten. Spencer Knight kept the Blackhawks in it as long as he could, stopping 25 of 28. The Wings moved to 6-4-3 and climbed off the bottom of the Atlantic to seventh, while the Blackhawks fell to 4-9-0, still last in the Central.

- Old team: Patrick Kane played his first game back in Detroit.
- Road run: The Blackhawks have lost five straight on the road.
- Lineup: NHL.com had projected the Red Wings with 11 forwards and seven defensemen, but they dressed 12 and six.
