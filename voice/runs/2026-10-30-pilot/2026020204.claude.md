# The Lightning hold off a late Jets push

Tampa Bay scored four times in the second, three of them in the last 4:04, to lead 5-1, and Winnipeg pulled Stuart Skinner after the period. Then the Jets made a game of it: Dylan Samberg scored his first of the season, Neal Pionk got his second of the night, and with their goalie pulled, Kyle Connor cut it to 5-4 with 2:48 left. Pontus Holmberg's empty-netter settled it.

Holmberg had a goal and three assists, and Brandon Hagel and Anthony Cirelli each had a goal and two. Cirelli's goal moved him past Brad Richards for ninth on the Lightning's all-time goals list. Pionk scored twice in his 600th NHL game.

- Slump: The Jets (4-6-3) are winless in seven (0-5-2).
- Standings: The Lightning (8-3-1) moved up to third in the Atlantic.
