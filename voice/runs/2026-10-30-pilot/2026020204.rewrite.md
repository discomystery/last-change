# The Lightning survive a scare

This one got way too interesting for the Lightning, and Neal Pionk, who scores in about 1 game in 12, was the reason, with two goals. Brayden Point got the scoring started for the Lightning early in the first, but Pionk tied it for Winnipeg late in the period, in his 600th NHL game. The second period belonged to Tampa Bay. Conor Geekie put the Lightning ahead for good midway through it, Anthony Cirelli and John Carlson scored a little over two minutes apart late in the period to make it 4-1, and Brandon Hagel added a fifth 56 seconds later, with Cirelli picking up his 200th NHL assist on the goal. That one stood up as the winner, because the third period was a wild one. Winnipeg pulled Stuart Skinner after five goals and started the third with Nico Daws, and Dylan Samberg and Pionk scored just under two minutes apart early in the period to cut the lead to 5-3. With the goalie pulled and less than three minutes left, Kyle Connor made it 5-4, before Pontus Holmberg shut the door with an empty-netter with a minute and a half left. Holmberg finished with four points on a goal and three assists, and Cirelli had a goal and two assists.

The Lightning carried the play, outshooting the Jets 31-21, and got the result to match, with Andrei Vasilevskiy making 17 saves for the Bolts. The Lightning moved to 8-3-1 and climbed to third in the Atlantic, while the Jets dropped to 4-6-3, seventh in the Central.

- Club history: Anthony Cirelli passed Brad Richards for ninth on the Lightning’s all-time goals list, with 151.
- Slump: The Jets are winless in seven (0-5-2).
- Finally: Dylan Samberg scored his first goal of the season, his first since Mar 3 (34 games played since).
- Snapped: The Lightning bounced back after two straight losses.
