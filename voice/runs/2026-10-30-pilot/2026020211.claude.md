# The Sabres bring a shutout into Anaheim

Buffalo blanked San Jose 2-0 last time out, and Colten Ellis is expected back in net. Otherwise it has been a choppy stretch: the Sabres are 4-3-3 in their last ten, with three of those losses coming after regulation. The Ducks are 3-6-1 over the same stretch and come home off a 6-5 overtime loss to Vancouver.

This one should be physical at one end only. Anaheim hits more than almost anyone, third in the league in our hit score, while Buffalo is 26th. At home, Joel Quenneville likes to send his second pair, Hinds and Luneau, out against the other team's second line. And Olen Zellweger faces the Ducks for the first time since leaving them.

Buffalo has been getting the better of the chances lately, so the Ducks need something to tip it, and a power play that gets going is their best bet.

- Out: The Ducks are newly without Pavel Mintyukov and Nikita Klepov, and Troy Terry hasn't played all season. The Sabres are newly without Konsta Helenius, and Alex Lyon hasn't played all season.
- Hot streak: Jackson LaCombe has a point in four straight games.
