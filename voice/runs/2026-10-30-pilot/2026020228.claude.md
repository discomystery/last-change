# The Capitals' sharp shooting meets the Wild's stingy defense

Washington has won three straight and is 7-3-0 in its last ten. Minnesota is 6-2-2 over the same stretch and won three in a row on the road before a 4-3 overtime loss to the Islanders.

The matchup to watch is quality against quality. The average Capitals shot has a 7.1% chance of going in, the best in the league, and the Wild are seventh at keeping shots harmless. Spencer Carbery likes to put Protas, Dubois and Wilson out against the other team's top line at home, 61% of the time when no matching would give about 36%. At the other end, Minnesota's power play (10th) gets a Washington penalty kill that ranks 27th.

Kirill Kaprizov is one point from 500, and Quinn Hughes has a point in six straight games against Washington.

- Out: The Wild are without Marcus Foligno, Filip Gustavsson and Brock Faber, so Jesper Wallstedt is expected to start. The Capitals have been without Rasmus Sandin for several games.
