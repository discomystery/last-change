# Kochetkov goes for ten straight in Nashville

Pyotr Kochetkov has won all nine of his starts this season, already the best season-opening run by a goalie in Hurricanes history, and he is expected in net again. Carolina has won three straight and is 8-1-1 in its last ten. Nashville is winless in two, and five of its 13 games have ended in a loss after regulation.

Expect the Hurricanes to shoot a lot. They are first in the league in our shot volume score, and the Predators are 27th at limiting shots. Sebastian Aho has six goals in six games against Nashville since 2023-24, and Jackson Blake and Nikolaj Ehlers both have points in five straight.

Carolina has been out-chancing opponents by a wide margin lately, so Nashville's best way through is getting good looks of its own.

- Out: The Hurricanes have been without Taylor Hall since Oct 13.
