# The Jets try to stop the slide in Florida, a night after Tampa

The Jets come in winless in seven (0-5-2) and on the second night of a back-to-back, after a 6-4 loss in Tampa Bay last night. At 4-6-3 they sit seventh in the Central and 26th in the league in points. The Panthers (7-4-2, fifth in the Atlantic) have won two straight, and the second of those was a 5-1 win in Winnipeg on Tuesday, the only meeting so far this season. Florida has also had two days off.

The Panthers are the league's most physical team: first in our hit score, first in forecheck pressure and first in rough stuff, so expect a chippy, heavy game. They also keep shots away better than almost anyone, allowing 24.2 shots a game (third fewest), and their penalty kill ranks sixth at 85.5%. The Jets don't block much and spend a lot of time pinned in their own end on long shifts, and they don't create many dangerous chances either: only three teams average a lower chance of scoring per shot. Winnipeg has scored 2.46 goals a game, 28th in the league.

Kyle Connor is carrying the Jets' offense with ten goals and 14 points, and Mark Scheifele, who has five goals in seven games against Florida since 2023-24, has nine assists. Florida's scoring runs through Matthew Tkachuk (four goals, 16 assists, nine points in his last five games) and Brady Tkachuk, who has 15 points and a point in five straight games against Winnipeg. The Panthers are still without Aleksander Barkov, Brad Marchand and Jacob Markström, and the Jets have been without Josh Morrissey since Oct 7.

Akira Schmid is expected to start for Florida (4-2-1, .885), and Stuart Skinner for Winnipeg (3-3-3, .899).

The special-teams gap is one to watch: Florida's power play is sixth in our ratings and scoring on 23% of its chances, while Winnipeg's kill is 23rd at 73.5%. At home, Paul Maurice likes to send Aaron Ekblad and Gustav Forsling out against the other team's top line, so expect them to see a lot of Connor, Scheifele and Cole Perfetti. The Jets' way back in is keeping dangerous shots away.
