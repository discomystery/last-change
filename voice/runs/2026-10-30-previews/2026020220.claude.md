# The Oilers bring the league's best power play to the Islanders

The Oilers are 9-2-2, first in the Pacific, and a perfect 5-0-0 on the road. They score more than anyone, 4.23 goals a game, and their power play leads the league at 35.7%. The Islanders are 7-4-2, fourth in the Metropolitan, have won two straight and are 4-1-1 at home. This is the first meeting of the season.

Edmonton leans on its stars more than any team in the league: no team gives its bottom six less of the ice time. The Oilers' defensemen join the attack, third in the league in the share of shots from the blue line, though the Islanders are first. New York's own power play has scored on just 17.5% of its chances, but its penalty kill is fifth at 86.4%, and it wins 55% of its faceoffs, second in the league. The Islanders' weak spot is giving up dangerous chances right after losing the puck, where they rank last. Both teams hit a lot, sixth and eighth in our hit score.

Connor McDavid has 36 points, 28 of them assists, and a point in every game this season, with 13 in his last five. Leon Draisaitl has 12 goals and seven goals in six games against the Islanders since 2023-24, and Evan Bouchard has 22 points from the blue line. The Islanders' leaders are Bo Horvat and Matthew Schaefer with 14 points each; Schaefer has seven in his last five. The Oilers are newly without Zach Hyman and have been without Matt Savoie all season. The Islanders have been without Matias Maccelli since Oct 17 and Mathew Barzal all season, and counting how much each missing player usually brings, only the Maple Leafs are missing more right now.

Tristan Jarry (3-1-2, .889) is expected to start for Edmonton, and Ilya Sorokin (6-3-0, .915) for the Islanders. Sorokin has been a wall against the Oilers, a .943 save percentage in five starts since 2023-24, though a goalie's record against one team says little about the next meeting.

At home, Peter DeBoer likes to send Tony DeAngelo and Matthew Kessel out against the other team's fourth line. The Islanders can tip it if they keep shots to a minimum.
